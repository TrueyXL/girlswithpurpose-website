About us

Every girl has a purpose

Girls With Purpose is a non-profit that empowers young girls and builds their confidence. We bring girls aged 7 to 10 together once a week to create, move, talk and cheer each other on.

We believe confidence is built by doing. So every week the girls try something new, speak up in front of the group, and leave knowing a little more about who they are and what they can do.

The 6 C’s we build

Everything we do each week comes back to six things we want every girl to leave with.

C01

Confidence

Believing in herself and feeling good about who she is.

C02

Competence

Learning real skills, from cooking and crafts to speaking in front of a group.

C03

Connection

Building true friendships and feeling that she belongs.

C04

Character

Knowing right from wrong and standing by her values.

C05

Caring

Showing kindness and empathy to the people around her.

C06

Contribution

Giving back to her family, her friends and her community.

The 6 C’s come from the Positive Youth Development model used by youth programs like 4-H.

How it works

Once a week

  1. Sign up for a session

    Each session runs 8 to 10 weeks. Fill in the form and send the $70 registration by e-transfer.

  2. Meet once a week

    The girls meet on one set day each week at 259 Boul. D’Anjou in Châteauguay.

  3. Celebrate together

    Every session ends with a celebration where the girls share what they made and learned.

Good to know

Questions parents ask

Who is Girls With Purpose for?

Girls aged 7 to 10. No experience needed, just come as you are.

Where do you meet?

259 Boul. D’Anjou, Châteauguay, QC J6J 2R4. Open in Google Maps.

How long is a session?

Each session runs 8 to 10 weeks, and the girls meet once a week on a set day. We’ll confirm the day and time by email when you register.

How much does it cost and how do I pay?

Registration is $70 per session, paid by Interac e-transfer to mwcn2016@gmail.com. Please write your daughter’s name in the message.

Will photos of my daughter be posted?

Only if you say yes on the registration form. You choose, and you can change your mind at any time by emailing us.

What do the girls do each week?

A mix of crafts, cooking, games, movement and group talks about friendship, kindness and confidence. Take a look at our previous sessions.
